# Mechanical Design Basics (C4) - Part 2 learner files

Free files for Part 2, "Drawings, tolerances & GD&T" (episodes 09-16). Read this first.

**No printing needed.** A squared notebook, a pencil and a calculator are enough. The spreadsheets are optional: work in Excel, LibreOffice or Google Sheets.

Your exercise part for the whole course is the shelf bracket: keep using the variant you picked in Part 1 (Shelf_Bracket_Brief.pdf is in the Part 1 files).
