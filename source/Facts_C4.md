Pulley movement fails 1.5 mm and passes 2.0 mm; shaft tilt (PDS R11) fails for both flat aluminium sheets. Ep 06 compares three routes to stiffness: thicker aluminium (3.0 mm; no standard M3 motor screw then works), a stiffer material (2.5 mm steel, flat; 2.0 mm steel passes the bracket check but leaves almost nothing once the motor shaft flexes) and shape (2.0 mm aluminium + lips; thin margin once the motor shaft flex is added). The Ep 07 matrix, with stiffness scored from the real margin and the heat path as a criterion, picks steel. The released ACT-103 is flat 2.5 mm low-carbon steel, zinc plated; the heat path is confirmed by a motor-face temperature test. All loads, limits, scores and costs are illustrative.
